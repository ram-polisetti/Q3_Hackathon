# Q3 Hackathon — Quantum Computing Entries

Qiskit entries for the Q3 quantum computing hackathon: reversible arithmetic circuits and trial notebooks.

## Contents

- `Problem_1/` — qubit-aware reversible adder (`qubit_aware_reversible_adder.py`) with circuit diagrams
- `Track1_trials/` — trial notebooks and experiments
  - `Reversiblebinaryadder/` — reversible binary adder work (`real_qubit_aware_reversible_adder.py`, notebooks, notes)
  - `Palindrome/` — palindrome experiment notebook

## Research use

Two files from this repository are licensed under MIT to Yonsei University's HAIR Lab for their **Parallel Quantum Instruction Dataset (PQID)** (manuscript under revision at *Scientific Data*):

- `Problem_1/qubit_aware_reversible_adder.py`
- `Track1_trials/Reversiblebinaryadder/real_qubit_aware_reversible_adder.py`
